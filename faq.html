ORIZBOSS FAQ

Everything you need to know about our lightning-fast service & delicious meals.

What are your operating hours?
We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Express delivery orders can be placed up to 10:30 PM.
How long does express food delivery take?
We prioritize speed! Most local orders arrive within 25 to 40 minutes after order placement, served hot and fresh.
Do you cater to special dietary restrictions?
Yes! We offer vegetarian, gluten-free, and vegan-friendly meal choices. Simply add special instructions in your checkout form.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash on Delivery for valid addresses.
Can I track my order status in real time?
Absolutely. Once signed into your ORIZBOSS account, you can track live updates on kitchen preparation and driver location right from your dashboard.
